# ePC ResNet-18 validation — 2026-08-14

The preregistered aggregate gate **failed on accuracy only**. ePC reached the
120-step sPC energy target in 2 steps and trained 6.65x faster per epoch, but
its three-seed mean CIFAR-10 accuracy was 5.39 percentage points lower.

## Protocol

- Working tree: branch `dmacd-epc-tw`, base commit
  `889012682392c26857879d72f04b82444bc0e7a8`, with the uncommitted ePC
  implementation under test.
- ResNet-18: 31 nodes, 38 edges, 2,795,210 parameters, ReLU, no augmentation.
- Paired seeds: 0, 1000, and 2000. Both arms saw the same shuffled batch stream
  within a seed.
- ePC: 10 inference steps at eta 0.1. sPC: 120 clipped inference steps at eta
  0.1. Both used AdamW, peak learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.01, batch size
  256, and two epochs.
- Training times include first-batch JIT compilation. Convergence step times
  exclude compilation through a warm-up call.
- The gate was evaluated on aggregate means, with no post-result
  hyperparameter tuning.

first release is intentionally DAG-only: ePC rejects repeated/back-edge
schedules, while cyclic unrolling remains an sPC feature. Tests cover that
rejection, and the cyclic MNIST validation below exercises the supported sPC
cycle path.

## Correctness-only follow-up

No tuning was performed after the failed gate. A fixed-parameter probe on the
first eight CIFAR-10 test examples ran the same ResNet for 120 ePC steps and
checked the resulting state and gradients:

- energy moved from 11.8880587 to 8.4980993;
- one additional ePC step changed energy by only `1.62e-5` in absolute value;
- relaxed-gradient RMS was `5.87e-5` over 3,442,688 elements;
- the maximum violation of `z_latent = z_mu + error` was `9.54e-7`;
- all 42 parameter-gradient leaves were finite.

Together with the independent gradient oracle, convex fixed-point equivalence,
and jitted end-to-end tests, the probe did not identify a correctness defect to
repair. The accuracy loss is therefore recorded as the empirical result of the
fixed 10-step ePC configuration, rather than hidden by hyperparameter changes.

The preregistered validation-selected, time-budgeted follow-up is documented in
[2026-08-14-time-accuracy.md](./2026-08-14-time-accuracy.md). It found that ePC
retained a 1.39x training-time advantage but suffered a repeatable
long-horizon training collapse under the selected cosine schedule.
